About Me

My name is Sruthi Ramesh and I'm a journalist based in Columbia, Missouri. I attend the University of Missouri and also work at KOMU 8 News, an NBC-Affiliate TV news station. In my four years at KOMU 8, I have worked in various positions. Now, I mainly work as an Executive Producer and Senior Digital Producer. As an Executive Producer, I'm responsible for managing a set of producers, and making sure their writing and newscasts are TV-ready. I also help manage breaking news and collaborate with reporters. As a Senior Digital Producer, I help manage the station's website content, write breaking news articles, edit web stories written by our digital team and reporters, and create social media content for Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok.
Outside of work, I'm a huge cinephile and love watching movies and TV shows (and rewatching them). The movie theater is one of my favorite places to be, and I'm always down to talk about new releases and old favorites. I also love music and going to concerts and festivals, like Bonnaroo in Tennessee.
